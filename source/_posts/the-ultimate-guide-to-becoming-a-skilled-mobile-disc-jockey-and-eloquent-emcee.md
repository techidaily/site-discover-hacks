## The Ultimate Guide to Becoming a Skilled Mobile Disc Jockey & Eloquent Emcee

In episode 8 of **Mobile DJ Tips with Bunn DJ Company** above, produced by our friends at the [BPM Supreme Record Pool](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), Joe Bunn explains how to step up your MC skills with a few solid tips.

![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/poster-mano-con-microfono-musica-simbolica.jpg?fit=300%2C250&ssl=1 "Hand with microphone, symbolic music")

While most Mobile DJs learn how to mix music or music videos confidently well before their first public performance, many find that being a quality MC in front of an audience is one of the most challenging parts of the job.

Speaking in front of a crowd can be nerve wracking, but if you’re properly prepared by doing your homework, have the right equipment for the job, and know some of the nuances such as how to properly hold your microphone, you can ease a lot of situational stress and MC like a veteran.

If you’re a Wedding DJ or Mobile, learning how to be a good MC is paramount to putting on a prestigious event. Watch the video above for Joe’s expert advice!

_**About The Video Author:** Joe Bunn is the founder and owner of Bunn DJ Company. He books over 800 weddings a year, plus another 400 private, corporate, and charity events. He’s also tutored DJs at industry events like Mobile Beat Las Vegas and DJ Times Expo._
